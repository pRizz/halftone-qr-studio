HALFTONE QR STUDIO - TEMPLATE IMAGE CREDITS

These image assets have their own public-domain/CC0 status, separate from
the application code's MIT license. Source pages were checked 2026-11-08.
Full images are served locally as 2048 x 2048 WebP files (quality 90), with
160 x 160 WebP thumbnails (quality 80). Metadata was removed. Photographs
were center-cropped to a square. The application can further crop, recolor,
dither, and overlay QR marks according to the user's chosen settings.
